CheckHER - Changes (November 3, 2026)
Bug fixes, Supplement Reminder (prototype page 20), Meal Guide (page 21)


BUG FIXES
---------
1. Result screen link
   "Nearby Health Services" opened a "Coming soon" page. It now opens the real
   Nearby Health Services screen.

2. Retake assessment
   Once taken, the assessment could only show the saved result. The result
   screen opened from the Menu now has a "Retake Assessment" button. Backing out
   of a retake returns to the saved result.

3. Blank page after the assessment
   Pressing back after finishing the assessment from the Menu left a blank page.
   Leaving the last Menu page now always returns to the tab it was opened from.

4. Assessment back button
   Only about the bottom 12px of the button responded to taps because it was
   drawn partly outside its parent. It now sits just under the logo header and
   the whole button works. The title below it moved slightly as a result.

5. Removed the outdated "TODO: Computation" comment in lib/content/assessment.dart.


SUPPLEMENT REMINDER (Menu > Supplement Reminder)
------------------------------------------------
- Next Reminder card, moves on as doses pass and shows "Tomorrow" when today's
  have passed. Doses due at the same time show as "+N more".
- Today's Schedule with an on/off switch for each medication.
- Add, edit and delete medications (name and dose, optional instructions, time).
  Delete asks for confirmation.
- Starts empty; the prototype's sample medicines are not preloaded.
- Daily phone notifications that work with the app in the background:
  - Permission is requested when a reminder is first saved or switched on.
  - If permission is denied, the app says reminders won't alert.
  - Medicine details are hidden on the lock screen.
  - Reminders are cancelled on logout and set up again on the next app start.
  - New one-colour notification icon.
- Known limit: notifications can run a few minutes late (about 3 and 5 minutes
  in testing) because exact timing needs a restricted Android permission.


MEAL GUIDE (Menu > Meal Guide)
------------------------------
- Water, meal and daily calorie counters with arrows. Water and meals are saved
  per day; the calorie goal carries over to the next day.
- Five topic shortcuts and six recommended meal options with nutrition tags.
- PMOS friendly foods: 78 foods from prototype/font ng layout.pdf, with search
  and category filters (Fruits 21, Vegetables 18, Dairy 11, Protein 14,
  Oils & Fats 14). Each shows serving, nutrition per 100 g, key nutrients and
  why it helps.

Changes made to the food text from the PDF:
- Star Fruit: "auto-hide for kidney disease" note became a visible warning,
  because the app does not store kidney health information.
- Pomelo: "flag CYP3A4 interaction in app profile" note became a visible warning.
- Virgin Coconut Oil: "specify portioning in database" reworded for users.
- "PCOS" changed to "PMOS" to match the rest of the app.
- Added the "Protein" heading, which is missing in the PDF.
- Label "Implementation" shown as "Why it helps".
- "Potas" and "Mag" expanded to Potassium and Magnesium.
- Small fixes: "a essential" to "an essential", "glp-1" to "GLP-1", and missing
  full stops.
- Added a "not medical advice" note at the end of the food list.
